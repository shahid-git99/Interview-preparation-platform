The PrepIQ Story

Interview prep that
actually adapts to you.

PrepIQ was built as an internship project to explore how generative AI can replace generic mock interview tools with something truly personal.

Our Mission

Every candidate deserves prep that matches their level.

Generic interview prep is broken. You get the same questions whether you're a fresher or a five-year veteran, studying Computer Science or Biotech. PrepIQ fixes this by reading your actual profile before generating a single question.

By connecting your background — college, degree, domain, experience, and resume — to a Gemini-powered engine, PrepIQ generates contextual questions that feel like a real recruiter prepared them specifically for you.
